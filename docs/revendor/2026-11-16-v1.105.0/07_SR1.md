## The title-bar pass (rulings 3 and 4), per window

This addon draws two windows of its own with a title bar. Each carries one title-bar control, the close,
built by `Core.MakeCloseButton(parent, onClick, "LootHistory")` through `core/CoreSetup.lua`'s
`NS.MakeCloseButton` (and `modules/Browser.lua`'s `B:MakeCloseButton`). No `W.HeaderButtons` adoption:
the library's own close is the smaller change, and it is no change at all in this addon's code.

| Window | Title-bar controls | Tooltip | Rest | Hover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| History browser (`modules/Browser.lua`, `LootHistoryWindow`) | close | "Close" (`Tooltip.CloseTip`), above, flips below, in `gtip.title` | `close.rest` = `icon.tint` | `close.hot` |
| Export modal (`modules/Export.lua`, `LootHistoryExportWindow`) | close | the same | the same | the same |

- Not this addon's: the export copy window is the library's `W.CopyWindow`, the debug console, the sim
  panel, the perf panel and the skin picker are the library's windows, covered by `SR-L2` and `SR-L3`.
- The test badge beside the History title is a font string, not a control. The mode strips under the
  filter bar are body chrome, not a title bar; their `ANCHOR_BOTTOM` tooltips stay owed to the
  placers adoption (LibKa0s#92), as 05_SUMMARY.md records.
- Test: `tests/test_skin_chrome.lua`'s every-skin header case now also holds, per window and in every
  registered skin, that each title-bar control shows a non-empty tooltip in `gtip.title` that leave
