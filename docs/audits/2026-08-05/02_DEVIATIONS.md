## Summary

**MUST failures: 13** · **SHOULD failures: 9** · **Advisory (MAY / observation): 3**

**Verdict: major deviations** — but the shape matters more than the count. **Seven of the thirteen
MUSTs are one decision** (`LH-20 … LH-26`): the `performance` section is un-adopted, recorded in the
repo as a knowing `wont-do` (`docs/pending/LEDGER.md`, `LIBKA0S-17`) with real reasoning — this
addon has no hot path, and `suspend` would drop real loot during the measurement window.
`performance`'s adoption strength is nonetheless **MUST for the wiring**, explicitly independent of
whether the addon has a hot path, so the audit must record it. Its resolution is a **user decision**:
adopt the wiring, or take the contradiction upstream. Under v2.21.0 that decision now has a second
consequence it did not have at the last audit — the **release gate** (`automated-tests-§3`) requires
all four suites at `pass`, and `perf` is a permanent `skip` here. The standard grants exactly one
narrow exception for an addon that ships no `tests/perf.lua`, and it **MUST be stated in the release
notes**; nothing in this repo states it.

The six remaining MUSTs are ordinary and independent: two dead code paths that read as working
(`LH-27`, `LH-43`), two defaults-duplication findings of which one has **already drifted and is
user-visible** (`LH-28`, `LH-34`), a latent bus-clobber fallback (`LH-35`), and an unpinned load-list
derivation (`LH-29`).

Everything else is in strong shape and was checked, not assumed: the TOC field order is exact, the
three-place standards reference is complete, the root doc set is exactly three docs plus `LICENSE`,
`docs/complexity.md` is correctly absent, the `automated-tests` bundles are real and the complexity
numbers **reproduce to the digit**, `luacheck .` is 0/0, the suite is 579/579, anti-patterns #47 and
#48 are clear, and the Options stub's load-completing shape is correct rather than inconsistent.

## Notes on scope — what was checked and deliberately **not** flagged

- **The four un-wired-looking subsystems are compliance, not gaps.** There is no
  `modules/DebugLog.lua`, no widget-maker file, no dispatcher and no hand-written test framework in
  this repo **because they are `LibKa0s` modules**. What the addon owns — a descriptor plus a
  degradation stub per module, in `core/CoreSetup.lua`, `core/DebugLogSetup.lua`,
  `settings/OptionsSetup.lua` and `settings/Slash.lua` — is present and correct.
  **Anti-pattern #47 is clear**, and no "missing console / missing dispatcher" deviation is raised.
- **The Options stub's load-completing shape is correct.** `settings/OptionsSetup.lua:44-64`
  publishes `LSMValues` real-enough for file load and no-ops the rest, exactly as `options-ui-§1`'s
  documented exception prescribes. Flagging it would be a false positive.
- **`libs/LibKa0s/` is the whole ship folder** — both files of the multi-file Options major and both
  of the Perf major are present even though Perf is unwired — and the TOC lists the single aggregate
  `libs\LibKa0s\LibKa0s.xml` once, in `# Libraries` after Ace3. **Anti-pattern #48 is clear.**
- **The vendor `diff -r` is NOT RUN, not passed.** The sibling repo was out of scope for this run by
  explicit instruction. `#45` is therefore **unverified**, not clear. LH-40 records that the addon's
  in-suite substitute cannot close that gap on its own.
