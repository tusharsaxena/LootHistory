# 02 — Deviations

Audited against **Ka0s WoW Addon Standard v2.64.0 (2026-09-23)** (`WowAddonStandards` master `e68795f`), tree
`54b7cc7`. Prefix **`LH-`**, reused. `LH-01…LH-61` are spent across six prior audit runs and four review bundles;
new rows start at **LH-62**. Every figure here is the one in `03_EVIDENCE.md`, produced by the command recorded there.

**Grade is impact, not rule strength** (`AUDIT.md` step 5). A doc-only or config-only failure is Low or Info even where
the rule is a MUST, and the row still names the MUST. **One exception is taken on purpose:** `AUDIT.md` step 4 fixes the
re-vendor-ledger finding at **High** ("A tag vendored with no bundle naming it and no `## Documented deviations` row saying
why is a **High** finding"), so LH-62 carries that grade even though no user can reach it. The tension between the two
steps is recorded in `01_CURRENT_STATE.md`; the playbook's specific instruction is followed rather than second-guessed.
